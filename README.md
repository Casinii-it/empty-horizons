
# empty-horizons

1976, was the year when the Sputnik 2 took on space, in search of new planets.  It was made iconic for two reasons:

1. The discovery of new planets
2. The fact that the team onboard got lost as Graydon forgot to download his offline google maps

In the empty horizons, you will embark on a journey to bounce back home.
